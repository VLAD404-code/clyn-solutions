About Us

Keeping Zambia’s Mining & Transport Moving

C-LYN SOLUTIONS LIMITED is a trusted supplier of mining and automotive truck parts, heavy-duty equipment components, electrical spares and general trading products based in Kitwe, on the Copperbelt.

We understand the demands of the mining and transport industries. Downtime costs money. That’s why we stock a wide range of quality parts — from common consumables to hard-to-find components — and back them with knowledgeable service.

Whether you need truck spares, heavy equipment parts or electrical components, our team is ready to help you get the right solution, fast.
